Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Ponasa LLC handles information connected with ponasa.com. It applies only to this website and direct communications with Ponasa LLC.

Information we receive

This website has no account registration, application form, payment page, or user comment feature. If you contact us by email or telephone, we may receive your name, contact details, property information, and the contents of your message. Our website host may automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, requested page, referring page, and request date and time to deliver, maintain, and secure the site.

Cookies and analytics

Ponasa LLC does not currently place advertising or analytics cookies through this website. The interactive property map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser referrer. Stock links open Google Finance only when selected. Other linked services, including county property records and Zillow, apply their own privacy policies after you leave this site.

How information is used

We may use information to respond to inquiries, communicate about properties, evaluate business opportunities, maintain records, operate and improve the website, detect misuse, protect our rights, and comply with legal obligations.

Disclosure

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. We may disclose information to hosting and technology providers, professional advisers, contractors, business counterparties involved in a requested matter, government authorities when legally required, or a successor in a merger, sale, or reorganization. We ask service providers to use information only for the services they provide to us.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, our business records, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent directly to us, subject to legal and operational limits. Because this site does not use targeted advertising or sell personal information, it does not currently provide opt-out controls for those activities. Browser-based “Do Not Track” signals are not treated differently where no uniform response standard applies.

Children

This website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Privacy questions or requests may be sent to info@ponasa.com, called in to +1 (954) 600-6579, or mailed to Ponasa LLC, 4530 NE 6th Avenue, Oakland Park, FL 33334.
