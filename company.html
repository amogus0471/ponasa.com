Our story

A steady approach to real estate.

Ponasa grew through ownership, timing, and a close understanding of the properties it manages.

01

2004–2009

Starting in Southeast Florida

Ponasa was formed in March 2004. The first purchase was a 10-unit apartment building near Fort Lauderdale Airport, followed by apartments, homes, and warehouses in the area.

The first property was sold profitably before the 2008 financial crisis, reinforcing the importance of timing as well as location.

02

2009–2012

Buying through the downturn

When the Florida market fell, Ponasa put its experience and capital to work. Acquisitions came through short sales, foreclosures, REO opportunities, and owners who needed to sell.

That disciplined buying period created a diversified portfolio that could perform in both strong and difficult markets.

03

2012–2016

New markets and partnerships

Ponasa began working in Barranquilla, Colombia, partnering with local construction companies and advising on land and capital opportunities.

In Italy, the portfolio began exploring short-term rentals in Florence, where tourism created a different path to property income.

04

2016 to today

Focused ownership

Today, Ponasa owns properties for residential, commercial, and rental use. The company continues to prioritize dependable rental potential, careful management, and clear communication with the people it serves.

New opportunities are evaluated with the same long-term view: build through cash flow, care for the property, and stay ready for the next good acquisition.

Ponasa LLC owns and manages real estate in Broward County, Florida.
