About Ponasa

A real estate company shaped by market cycles and practical ownership experience.

Ponasa was formed in 2004 with a focus on Southeast Florida real estate. After the 2008 downturn, the company used its experience and resources to acquire properties through a disciplined process, building a diversified portfolio that still supports its position today.

As the company expanded, Ponasa developed activity in Colombia and Italy, pairing local market knowledge with a rental-first investment philosophy.
